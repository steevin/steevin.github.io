Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Data stored on your device

MemoTempo stores your preferred name, memories, plans, dates, reminder preferences and app settings locally on your device. MemoTempo does not require an account and the developer does not operate a server that receives this information.

2. Data collection and tracking

MemoTempo does not collect personal data, analytics, diagnostics or advertising identifiers. It does not sell data, show advertising or track you across apps and websites.

3. Optional Apple system features

If you enable reminders, Spotlight indexing or device authentication, iOS processes the information needed to provide those features. MemoTempo cannot access Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Where available, writing assistance uses Apple Intelligence on the device; your memory text is not sent to the developer.

4. Backups and sharing

A MemoTempo backup is created only when you choose Export Backup. You select where that file is saved or shared. If iCloud device backup is enabled, Apple may include local app data in your device backup under Apple’s privacy terms.

5. Deleting your data

You can archive individual memories or permanently delete all MemoTempo data from Settings. Removing the app also removes its local data, subject to any device backups managed by Apple.

6. Contact

For privacy or support questions, email soporte@steevin.com.

Resumen en español

MemoTempo guarda tu nombre preferido, recuerdos, planes y ajustes únicamente en tu dispositivo. No requiere una cuenta, no envía tus recuerdos a servidores del desarrollador y no usa publicidad, analítica ni seguimiento. Las funciones opcionales de Apple —recordatorios, Spotlight, autenticación del dispositivo y Apple Intelligence— son procesadas por iOS. Puedes exportar una copia o eliminar permanentemente todos tus datos desde Ajustes.
